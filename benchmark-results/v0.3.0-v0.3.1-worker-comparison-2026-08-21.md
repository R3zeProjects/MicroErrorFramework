# v0.3.0-beta to v0.3.1-beta WorkerPool comparison

Environment: Windows 10 Pro, AMD Ryzen 7 PRO 1700X, Clang 22.1.6, C++23,
Release. Values are medians of seven independent full-production runs with
alternating revision order and 200,000 operations per scenario.

WorkerPool scalar-dispatch geometric-mean gain: **+15.3%**. The geometric mean
over every WorkerPool scenario was **+9.7%**.

| Scenario | Producers | Workers | Baseline ops/s | Candidate ops/s | Delta |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| dispatch q64 | 1 | 1 | 4,212,860 | 4,375,810 | +3.9% |
| dispatch q64 | 1 | 4 | 2,987,830 | 3,350,060 | +12.1% |
| dispatch q64 | 4 | 4 | 2,477,980 | 2,955,900 | +19.3% |
| dispatch q64 | 4 | 16 | 1,840,150 | 2,201,850 | +19.7% |
| dispatch q1024 | 1 | 4 | 3,018,360 | 3,414,500 | +13.1% |
| dispatch q1024 | 4 | 1 | 3,068,130 | 3,115,290 | +1.5% |
| dispatch q1024 | 4 | 4 | 2,444,820 | 3,054,610 | +24.9% |
| dispatch q1024 | 4 | 16 | 1,906,070 | 2,236,360 | +17.3% |

| bulk | 1 | 4 | 12,120,300 | 11,069,800 | -8.7% |

| shutdown cancellation | 1 | 1 | 71,957 | 45,280 | -37.1% |

Backpressure changed by +1.3% at 1 microsecond and +0.2% at 20 microseconds.
CPU work changed by +3.1% at 256 iterations and +1.3% at 4,096 iterations.
The bulk and lifecycle cancellation regressions are retained explicitly rather
than being hidden by the aggregate. Cancellation throughput includes scheduler
timing around an intentionally sleeping active task and is not a dispatch gate.

The comparison tool and GitHub Action enforce:

- at least +5% WorkerPool scalar-dispatch geometric-mean gain;
- no scalar dispatch scenario below -15%;
- no subsystem geometric mean below -12.5%.

Raw CSV files are uploaded by GitHub Actions and are intentionally excluded from
the installed header-only package.
